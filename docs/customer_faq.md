# Olist Platform — Customer FAQ

## About Orders

**How do I place an order on Olist?**
Browse products using the search bar or category navigation. Add items to your cart, proceed to checkout, enter your delivery address, select a shipping method, choose your payment method, and confirm the order. You will receive an email confirmation with your order number immediately after checkout.

**Can I modify my order after placing it?**
Order modifications (changing items, quantities, or addresses) are only possible before the seller confirms and begins processing the order. Once the order status changes to "processing" or "dispatched," modifications are no longer possible. If you need to change your delivery address after dispatch, contact the carrier directly with your tracking code.

**Can I cancel my order?**
You may cancel an order at any time before the seller dispatches it. Once dispatched, cancellation is no longer possible, but you may initiate a return after receiving the product. To cancel, go to your order page and select "Cancel Order." If the seller has already dispatched the item, the cancel button will be unavailable and you will see the option to request a return instead.

**My order shows as delivered but I haven't received it. What should I do?**
If your order is marked delivered but you have not received it, first check with neighbors or building staff, as carriers sometimes leave packages at nearby locations. If the package cannot be located within 24 hours, contact Olist support through the order page. Do not wait more than 48 hours after the delivery notification, as this may affect your claim eligibility.

**How do I track my order?**
Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking code by email. You can track your order through the Olist app, the website order portal under "My Orders," or directly on the carrier's tracking website. Allow up to 1 business day after dispatch for the tracking information to update in the carrier's system.

## About Payments

**What payment methods does Olist accept?**
Olist accepts the following payment methods:

- **Credit Card (Cartão de Crédito)**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Elo, Hipercard. Purchases can be split into up to 12 interest-free installments depending on the seller's settings.
- **Boleto Bancário**: A bank slip (boleto) is generated at checkout and must be paid within 2 business days. Order processing begins only after payment confirmation, which may take up to 1 business day.
- **Debit Card (Cartão de Débito)**: Visa Electron and Maestro debit cards are accepted. Payment is processed in real time.
- **PIX**: Instant payment via Brazil's real-time payment system. Orders are confirmed immediately upon PIX payment.
- **Vouchers and Promotional Credits**: Platform vouchers issued by Olist can be applied at checkout. Multiple vouchers can be combined, but only one promotional code per order.

**What is the maximum number of installments available?**
Credit card purchases can be split into up to 12 installments. The installment options available depend on the individual seller's configuration and the total order value. Orders below R$50 typically cannot be split into more than 3 installments.

**Is it safe to pay on Olist?**
Yes. All payment processing on Olist uses SSL encryption and complies with PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard). Card details are never stored on Olist's servers; they are processed directly by our payment partners.

**I was charged but my order was not confirmed. What should I do?**
If your card was charged but you received no order confirmation, first check your spam folder for the confirmation email. If no email is found, check your Olist account under "My Orders." If no order appears, the charge was likely an authorization hold that will be released within 3 to 5 business days. Contact your bank or Olist support if the charge persists beyond 5 business days.

## About Products and Quality

**What warranty do products carry?**
All products sold on Olist are covered by the legal guarantee (garantia legal) mandated by Brazil's Consumer Defense Code:

- **30 days** for non-durable goods (consumable items)
- **90 days** for durable goods (electronics, appliances, tools, furniture)

This guarantee covers manufacturing defects and applies from the date of receipt. It is in addition to any manufacturer's warranty offered separately. Sellers must honor these warranty periods by law.

**What if I receive the wrong product or a product not as described?**
If you receive a different product from what was listed, or if the product has characteristics that significantly differ from the listing description, you are entitled to a full refund or replacement under the platform's return policy. The seller covers return shipping costs in these cases. Report the issue within 7 calendar days of receipt.

**How do I know if a seller is trustworthy?**
Each seller on Olist has a public profile showing their average review score, total number of sales, and response rate. Look for sellers with scores above 4.0 and a significant number of completed orders. Reviews from verified buyers are displayed on the product page.

## About Reviews

**How does the review system work?**
After your order is delivered, you will receive an invitation to rate your experience. Reviews consist of a score from 1 to 5 stars and an optional written comment. Reviews are published publicly on the product page and contribute to the seller's average performance score. You can submit only one review per order.

**Can I edit or delete my review?**
Reviews can be edited within 48 hours of submission. After that, they become permanent. Olist does not delete reviews unless they violate community guidelines (e.g., contain personal information, offensive language, or are demonstrably fraudulent).

**What should I do if a seller pressures me to change my review?**
Any seller who contacts you asking you to modify or remove a negative review in exchange for a refund, discount, or gift is violating Olist's seller code of conduct. You should report this behavior through the order page using the "Report Issue" button. Olist takes review manipulation seriously and may suspend or remove sellers found engaging in this practice.

## About Accounts and Privacy

**How do I update my delivery address?**
Go to Account Settings and select "Addresses." You can add new addresses or edit existing ones. Note that changing your saved address does not affect addresses on orders that have already been placed.

**Can I have multiple accounts?**
Each customer is permitted one Olist account per CPF. Duplicate accounts are detected automatically and may result in both accounts being suspended.

**How does Olist use my personal data?**
Olist processes your personal data in accordance with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados — LGPD). Your data is used to process orders, provide customer support, improve platform services, and (with your consent) send promotional communications. You may request a copy of your data, corrections, or deletion through the Privacy Center in Account Settings.
