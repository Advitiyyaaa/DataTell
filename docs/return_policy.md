# Olist Platform — Return & Refund Policy

## Overview

This policy governs all returns, refunds, and exchanges for products purchased through the Olist marketplace. It applies to all customers and sellers operating on the platform. Olist complies fully with Brazil's Consumer Defense Code (Código de Defesa do Consumidor — CDC), which provides consumers with statutory rights that cannot be waived by any seller.

## Standard Return Window

Customers may return any product within **7 calendar days** of receipt, provided the product is unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by the original invoice (nota fiscal). This is the standard cooling-off period (direito de arrependimento) guaranteed by Article 49 of the CDC for purchases made outside a physical establishment.

For products that arrive damaged, defective, or not as described in the listing, the return window is extended to **30 calendar days** from the date of receipt. Customers must report the issue within this period to be eligible for a full refund or replacement.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following categories are not eligible for return unless the product is defective or not as described:

- Perishable goods and food products
- Digital products and software licenses (once downloaded or activated)
- Personal hygiene and intimate apparel items that have been opened
- Custom or personalized products made to the buyer's specifications
- Hazardous materials or items whose return is restricted by law

## Electronics and Technical Products

Electronics, appliances, and technical products are covered by a **90-day legal guarantee** (garantia legal) as mandated by Article 26 of the CDC. This guarantee covers manufacturing defects and applies regardless of any additional warranty offered by the seller or manufacturer. If a defect is discovered within the 90-day window, the customer is entitled to:

1. Repair of the product at no cost
2. Replacement with an equivalent product
3. Full refund of the purchase price

Sellers are responsible for covering return shipping costs when the defect is confirmed.

## Return Process

To initiate a return:

1. Open a dispute through the Olist order management portal within the applicable return window
2. Select the reason for the return (changed mind, defective, wrong item, etc.)
3. Wait for seller approval, which must be granted within 3 business days
4. Once approved, ship the product using the provided return label
5. Keep the tracking code for your records

Sellers who fail to respond to return requests within 3 business days will have the return automatically approved by the platform.

## Refund Methods and Timelines

Refunds are processed according to the original payment method used for the purchase:

**Credit Card (Cartão de Crédito)**
Refunds to credit card are processed within 5 to 10 business days after the returned product is received and inspected by the seller. The charge reversal may appear on the customer's next billing cycle or the cycle after that, depending on the card issuer. In no case will the refund take longer than 2 billing cycles.

**Boleto Bancário**
Boleto payments are refunded via bank transfer (TED/PIX) to a bank account provided by the customer. The customer must submit their bank details through the Olist portal within 5 business days of the refund approval. The transfer is completed within 5 business days after receipt of bank details.

**Debit Card (Cartão de Débito)**
Debit card refunds are credited back to the originating card within 5 business days of refund approval.

**Vouchers and Promotional Credits**
Purchases made with platform vouchers are refunded as new vouchers with the same value and validity. Vouchers used in combination with other payment methods result in proportional refunds to each method.

## Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued in cases where:
- Only part of a multi-item order is returned
- The returned product shows signs of use beyond inspection (in which case a depreciation fee of up to 20% may apply)
- The return is initiated after the standard window but within the extended defect window

## Seller Responsibilities

Sellers are obligated to:
- Accept all valid return requests within the platform's defined windows
- Cover return shipping costs for defective or incorrectly described products
- Inspect returned goods within 5 business days of receipt
- Notify the platform immediately upon receipt and inspection
- Issue refunds promptly upon confirmation of the valid return

Sellers who repeatedly reject valid returns or delay the refund process are subject to performance penalties, including suspension from the platform.

## Platform Mediation

If a dispute cannot be resolved between the buyer and seller within 10 business days, Olist's customer protection team will mediate. The platform reserves the right to issue a refund directly to the customer and recover the amount from the seller's pending payouts if the seller is found to be at fault.
