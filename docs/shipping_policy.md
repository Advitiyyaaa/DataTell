# Olist Platform — Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Overview

This document describes the shipping and delivery standards that apply to all orders placed through the Olist marketplace. Both sellers and customers are expected to understand these terms. Shipping timelines, carrier responsibilities, and tracking procedures are governed by the agreements between Olist, its seller network, and contracted logistics partners.

## Standard Delivery Timeframes

Delivery times vary based on the customer's location, the seller's location, product size and weight, and selected shipping method. The following are estimated ranges under normal operating conditions:

**Standard Shipping (Frete Padrão)**
- Capital cities (São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Belo Horizonte, Brasília): 3 to 7 business days
- State capitals and major regional cities: 5 to 10 business days
- Interior municipalities and remote areas: 8 to 15 business days
- Northern and remote states (Amazonas, Roraima, Amapá, Acre): 10 to 20 business days

**Express Shipping (Frete Expresso)**
Express delivery is available for orders in major metropolitan areas:
- São Paulo metropolitan area: 1 to 2 business days
- Rio de Janeiro metropolitan area: 1 to 3 business days
- Other major capitals: 2 to 5 business days

Express shipping carries a surcharge that varies by product weight and destination and is displayed at checkout before purchase confirmation.

## Seller Dispatch Requirements

Sellers are required to dispatch orders within **3 business days** of order approval and payment confirmation. This dispatch deadline is strictly enforced by the platform:

- Day 0: Order approved and payment confirmed
- Day 1-3: Seller must pack and hand off to carrier
- Day 3 end-of-day: Final deadline for dispatch

If a seller fails to dispatch within 3 business days without prior notification to the customer, the customer has the right to cancel the order for a full refund. Repeated dispatch delays negatively affect the seller's performance score and may result in penalties.

## Shipping Costs

**Free Shipping Threshold**
Customers qualify for free standard shipping on orders with a total product value of R$79 or more for most product categories. Some categories with high freight costs (such as furniture, large appliances, and construction materials) have a higher free shipping threshold of R$199.

**Weight and Dimension Surcharges**
Products exceeding 30 kg or with cubic dimensions requiring freight calculation above standard rates will incur additional charges. These surcharges are calculated automatically at checkout based on the product dimensions provided by the seller.

**Multi-Seller Orders**
When an order contains products from multiple sellers, separate freight charges apply for each seller's shipment. Free shipping thresholds are evaluated per seller, not per total order value.

## Tracking and Notifications

All orders include tracking. Once a seller dispatches an order, the carrier provides a tracking code within 1 business day of pickup. Customers receive:

- Email notification when the order is dispatched by the seller
- SMS/push notification when the carrier updates the tracking status
- Delivery confirmation notification when the package is marked delivered

Customers can track their orders through the Olist app, the website order portal, or directly on the carrier's website using the provided tracking code.

## Delivery Attempt Policy

Carriers make up to **3 delivery attempts** at the address provided at checkout. Between attempts:

- A delivery notification (aviso de entrega) is left at the address
- The package is held at the nearest carrier branch for up to 7 calendar days
- If uncollected after 7 days, the package is returned to the seller

If the delivery fails due to an incorrect address provided by the customer, re-delivery shipping costs are the customer's responsibility. If the failure is due to carrier error, re-delivery is arranged at no additional cost.

## Damaged or Lost Shipments

**Damaged Packages**
If a package arrives visibly damaged, customers should:
1. Photograph the damage before opening the package
2. Note the damage on the carrier's delivery receipt if possible
3. Report the incident through Olist within 24 hours of delivery
4. Preserve all original packaging materials for potential carrier investigation

**Lost Shipments**
A shipment is considered lost if tracking shows no update for more than 10 business days or if the estimated delivery window has passed by more than 5 business days. Customers should open a claim through Olist, which will investigate with the carrier. If the shipment is confirmed lost, a full refund or replacement will be provided within 10 business days of the confirmed loss.

**Insurance**
All shipments through Olist's contracted carriers include basic insurance covering the declared product value. For high-value orders above R$1,000, additional insurance is automatically applied.

## International Shipping

Olist currently operates within Brazil only. Orders cannot be shipped to addresses outside Brazilian territory. Customers with international addresses are not eligible to complete a purchase on the platform.

## Shipping During Peak Periods

During high-demand periods (Black Friday, Christmas, and Carnival season), delivery timeframes may extend by 3 to 5 additional business days beyond the standard estimates. Sellers are expected to update their available stock and processing capacity during these periods. Customers are encouraged to place orders well in advance of gift-giving dates.
