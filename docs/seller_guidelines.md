# Olist Platform — Seller Guidelines & Performance Standards

## Overview

This document defines the operational requirements, performance standards, and code of conduct for all sellers operating on the Olist marketplace. Compliance with these guidelines is mandatory and forms the basis of the seller agreement. Failure to meet performance standards may result in warnings, penalties, or permanent removal from the platform.

## Seller Onboarding Requirements

To become an active seller on Olist, applicants must provide:

- Valid CNPJ (Cadastro Nacional da Pessoa Jurídica) for companies, or CPF for individual sellers (MEI)
- Brazilian bank account in the name of the registered entity
- Proof of address (comprovante de residência) dated within the last 90 days
- Complete product catalog with accurate descriptions, photographs, and pricing
- Agreement to the Olist Terms of Service and this Seller Guidelines document

Sellers are approved within 3 to 5 business days of submitting complete documentation. Incomplete submissions reset the review clock.

## Performance Metrics

Olist evaluates seller performance using four primary metrics, reviewed on a rolling 90-day basis:

**1. Average Review Score (ARS)**
The weighted average of all customer review scores received in the past 90 days. Each review is scored from 1 to 5. Scores from the most recent 30 days carry double weight in the calculation.

- Target: 4.0 or above
- Minimum acceptable: 3.5
- Warning threshold: below 3.5 for 30 consecutive days
- Suspension threshold: below 3.0 or below 3.5 for 60 consecutive days

**2. On-Time Dispatch Rate (OTDR)**
The percentage of orders dispatched within the required 3-business-day window.

- Target: 95% or above
- Minimum acceptable: 90%
- Warning threshold: below 90% in any 30-day window
- Suspension threshold: below 80% for two consecutive 30-day windows

**3. Order Cancellation Rate (OCR)**
The percentage of seller-initiated order cancellations. Customer-initiated cancellations (before dispatch) do not count against this metric.

- Target: below 2%
- Maximum acceptable: below 5%
- Warning threshold: 5% to 10%
- Suspension threshold: above 10% in any 30-day window

**4. Customer Message Response Time (CMRT)**
The average time to first response to customer messages, measured in hours.

- Target: within 12 hours
- Maximum acceptable: within 48 hours
- Warning threshold: average response time above 48 hours
- Suspension threshold: average response time above 72 hours or no response on more than 20% of messages

## Performance Review Process

Performance is evaluated continuously. When a seller falls below the minimum acceptable threshold for any metric:

1. **Day 1**: Automated warning notification sent to seller's registered email and dashboard
2. **Day 15**: If no improvement, secondary warning with a required remediation plan submission
3. **Day 30**: Account review by Olist's Seller Success team
4. **Day 45**: If metric remains below threshold, temporary suspension with a 30-day improvement window
5. **Day 75**: If no recovery, permanent removal from the platform

Sellers in suspension cannot receive new orders but must continue to fulfill all orders placed before the suspension date.

## Conduct Standards

**Product Listings**
Sellers must provide accurate and complete product descriptions. The following are strictly prohibited:
- Misleading claims about product quality, origin, or specifications
- Counterfeit or grey-market goods
- Products in prohibited categories (as defined in the Olist Prohibited Items Policy)
- Duplicate listings of the same product to artificially inflate search ranking
- Keyword stuffing in product titles or descriptions

**Customer Interactions**
Sellers must maintain professional and respectful communication with customers at all times. The following will result in immediate account review:
- Threatening or abusive language in any customer communication
- Attempts to conduct transactions outside the platform to avoid fees
- Soliciting customers to remove or change negative reviews
- Offering discounts or gifts in exchange for positive review scores

**Pricing**
Sellers may not engage in price manipulation, including:
- Artificially inflating prices before promotional periods to exaggerate discounts
- Price gouging during high-demand or emergency situations
- Coordinating pricing with other sellers in a manner that constitutes anti-competitive behavior

## Payouts and Fees

Olist charges a commission on each completed sale, which varies by product category (typically between 8% and 16%). Payouts to sellers are released according to the following schedule:

- Orders with status "delivered" and no active disputes: payment released 14 days after confirmed delivery
- Orders where the customer confirms receipt manually: payment released immediately after confirmation
- Orders with active return or refund disputes: payment held until resolution

Sellers can view their pending and released payments in the Seller Portal dashboard.

## Prohibited Products and Restricted Categories

The following product types are prohibited from being sold on Olist under any circumstances:
- Weapons, ammunition, and related accessories
- Illegal drugs and controlled substances
- Products that infringe intellectual property rights (counterfeit goods)
- Human organs or biological materials
- Endangered species or products derived from them

Restricted categories (requiring special documentation) include:
- Pharmaceuticals and health supplements
- Financial instruments and investment products
- Items requiring technical certification (ANATEL, INMETRO approval)

## Review and Appeals Process

Sellers who believe a performance warning or suspension is unjust may submit a formal appeal within 10 business days of the notification. Appeals are reviewed by Olist's Seller Relations team within 5 business days. During the appeal period, the seller's account status remains unchanged. If the appeal is successful, the warning or suspension is removed and the relevant data point is excluded from the performance calculation.
